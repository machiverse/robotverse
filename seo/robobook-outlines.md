# RoboBook Article Outlines — Phase 4

_Three SEO-focused RoboBook article outlines for RobotVerse. These posts are designed for an Indian B2B industrial robotics marketplace with relatively new domain authority, so each topic emphasizes realistic long-tail or comparison intent, clear internal-link pathways, and strong commercial assist value._

_Use `Article` / `BlogPosting` schema on all posts. `FAQPage` and `HowTo` markup may still be added where structurally accurate, but they should be treated as supporting markup rather than a guaranteed rich-result strategy because Google has reduced FAQ and HowTo rich-result visibility for most sites.[cite:77][cite:79] JSON-LD is the preferred implementation format.[cite:88]_

## Editorial rules for all RoboBook posts

- Each article should target **one clear primary keyword theme**.
- Each post should link naturally to:
  - one brand, payload, application, or guide hub page,
  - 2–4 relevant marketplace pages,
  - and one commercial CTA path.
- Avoid unsupported claims such as “best-selling” or “most requested” unless backed by actual RobotVerse inventory or inquiry data.
- Every post should add original value beyond generic SEO copy:
  - Indian market context,
  - practical buying guidance,
  - inspection or sourcing advice,
  - and links to relevant inventory or service pages.
- If structured data is implemented, it must match visible on-page content and comply with Google’s general structured-data quality guidance.[cite:75]
